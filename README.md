# SWYNEX Biological Dataset Selection

**Submitted by:** Meghana S
**Institution:** JSS Academy of Higher Education & Research, Mysuru

## Selected Dataset
Gene expression signature of endometrial samples from women with and without endometriosis

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Source | NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) |
| Accession | GSE135485 |
| Link | https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE135485 |
| Organism | Homo sapiens |
| Technology | RNA-seq, Illumina HiSeq 3000 (GPL21290) |
| Total samples | 58 (54 endometriosis patients, 4 healthy controls) |

## Research Question
Which genes differ significantly between endometriosis samples and healthy endometrium, and can they point to candidate biomarkers?

## Objectives
1. Identify differentially expressed genes.
2. Compare upregulated and downregulated genes.
3. Understand molecular changes linked to endometriosis.
4. Identify candidate genes for further investigation.

## Why this dataset
- Purely computational (dry-lab) study, no wet-lab work needed.
- Public, well-annotated, with a clear patient vs. control comparison.

## Limitation
Only 4 healthy controls, so results are exploratory.

## Files
- `bioinformatics_projects.xlsx`: differential expression results with upregulated and downregulated gene sheets.

## Reference
NCBI GEO, accession GSE135485.
